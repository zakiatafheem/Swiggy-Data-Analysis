# Swiggy Data Analysis Project

## 📌 Overview

This project focuses on performing **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on Swiggy restaurant data to uncover insights related to pricing, customer preferences, restaurant performance, and platform-level opportunities.

The goal is to transform raw data into meaningful insights that can drive better business decisions for both restaurants and the Swiggy platform.

---

## 📊 Objectives

* Understand customer ordering patterns
* Analyze pricing vs ratings relationship
* Identify popular cuisines and locations
* Extract actionable insights for restaurants and Swiggy

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used

* Python
* Pandas
* NumPy
* Matplotlib
* Seaborn

---

## 📊 Key Insights
🔹 Customer Ratings

Most restaurants fall within a moderate rating range, indicating consistent but highly competitive service quality.

🔹 Pricing Trends

The majority of restaurants operate in the mid-price range, showing that affordability plays a major role in customer engagement.

🔹 Regional Food Trends 

Food choices vary significantly across states — certain dishes clearly dominate specific regions, highlighting localized demand patterns.

🔹 Price vs Rating

Higher prices do not necessarily lead to better ratings, emphasizing the importance of value-for-money.

🔹 Customer Engagement (Rating Count)

A small number of restaurants receive a disproportionately high number of ratings, indicating uneven visibility and demand.

🔹 Outlier Analysis

Significant skewness in pricing and rating counts highlights the importance of proper data cleaning before drawing conclusions.

---

## 🍽️ Recommendations for Restaurants

* Focus on mid-range pricing strategies
* Improve low-rated menu items
* Leverage popular cuisines to attract more customers
* Offer combo meals in less popular categories
* Place top-selling items at the top of the menu

---

## 🚀 Recommendations for Swiggy

* Increase supply in high-demand, low-supply areas
* Promote high-performing categories during weekends
* Optimize delivery pricing in high-demand cities
* Use personalized recommendations based on user behavior

---

## 📌 Conclusion

This project demonstrates how data analysis can provide valuable insights into customer behavior and business strategy. Even simple EDA can lead to impactful decisions when interpreted correctly.
