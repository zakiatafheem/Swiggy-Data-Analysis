# Swiggy Data Analysis Project

## 📌 Overview

This project focuses on performing **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on Swiggy restaurant data to uncover insights related to pricing, customer preferences, restaurant performance, and platform-level opportunities.

The goal is to transform raw data into meaningful insights that can drive better business decisions for both restaurants and the Swiggy platform.

---

## 📊 Objectives

* Understand customer ordering patterns
* Analyze pricing vs ratings relationship
* Identify popular cuisines and locations
* Extract actionable insights for restaurants and Swiggy

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used

* Python
* Pandas
* NumPy
* Matplotlib
* Seaborn

---

## 📊 Key Insights

* Mid-range restaurants tend to perform better in terms of ratings and engagement
* Certain cuisines dominate specific locations
* Higher discounts do not always lead to higher profitability
* A small number of restaurants contribute to a large portion of orders

---

## 🍽️ Recommendations for Restaurants

* Focus on mid-range pricing strategies
* Improve low-rated menu items
* Leverage popular cuisines to attract more customers
* Offer combo meals in less popular categories
* Place top-selling items at the top of the menu

---

## 🚀 Recommendations for Swiggy

* Increase supply in high-demand, low-supply areas
* Promote high-performing categories during weekends
* Optimize delivery pricing in high-demand cities
* Use personalized recommendations based on user behavior

---

## 📈 Visualizations

The project includes multiple visualizations such as:

* Histogram (ratings distribution)
* Count plots (cuisine popularity)
* Heatmaps (correlation analysis)
* Scatter plots (price vs rating)
